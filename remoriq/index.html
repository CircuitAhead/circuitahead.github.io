WHY REMORIQ

Your phone already holds your life. Finding it shouldn't be hard.

Messages, receipts, screenshots, PDFs, links, warranties, reminders and useful information end up scattered across different apps and folders.

REMORIQ is designed to give that information one place where it can become useful again.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN

Your memory should belong to you.

REMORIQ is designed as a local-first, accountless application. Your core content does not depend on a REMORIQ cloud or REMORIQ backend to remain useful.

✓

No REMORIQ account required No REMORIQ email/password or identity service is needed for core use.

✓

Core content stored locally The device is the primary system of record for normal use.

✓

Designed to work offline Ordinary core workflows do not require an always-on connection.

✓

Your data remains portable Export, backup, restore and migration are treated as user ownership features.
