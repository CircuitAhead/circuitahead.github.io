PRIVACY

Privacy information for Foundry Preflight Next.

Foundry Preflight Next is a local Windows companion utility. This page describes the beta project's current privacy approach and the role of external software used alongside it.

Local configuration

The beta is designed around local configuration and session-preparation data on the user's PC. CircuitAhead does not require a Foundry Preflight Next account for the beta workflow.

External services

Foundry VTT and ZeroTier are independent products. Their own services, accounts, networking and privacy practices are governed by their respective providers, not by CircuitAhead.

Support communications

If you contact CircuitAhead, the information you choose to send by email is used to understand and respond to your request. Avoid sending credentials or unrelated personal data.
