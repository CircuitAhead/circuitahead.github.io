MASTER & PLAYER

Different roles, different preparation.

MASTER

Host with fewer moving parts.

The Master workflow focuses on world protection, Foundry readiness, network state and connection prerequisites.

  • Reusable Master profile
  • World backup workflow
  • Firewall and connectivity verification
PLAYER

Join without rebuilding the setup.

The Player workflow keeps connection information and supported local backup actions in a simpler path.

  • Reusable Player profile
  • Persistent session link
  • ZeroTier readiness checks

DESIGN APPROACH

A companion tool, not another platform.

FoundryPreflightNext does not replace Foundry VTT or ZeroTier. It coordinates recurring preparation around them.

Local Windows workflow

Built around the user's own PC and local session setup.

Repeatable checks

Recurring preparation becomes a recognizable sequence.

Role-aware experience

Master and Player receive tools aligned with their responsibilities.
